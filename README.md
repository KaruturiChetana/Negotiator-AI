# Negotiator AI

## Overview
Negotiator AI is an AI-powered shopping and negotiation assistant focused on laptops and smartphones.

It helps users compare product offers, evaluate value for money, stay within budget, and prepare polite negotiation messages for sellers.

## Features
- Compare laptop and smartphone offers.
- Evaluate specifications, price, condition, and warranty.
- Identify the cheapest and best-value options.
- Suggest negotiation strategies and counteroffers.
- Draft polite messages to request discounts.
- Help buyers identify risks when purchasing used devices.
- Respect the user's maximum budget.

## How to Use
Share the product model, specifications, price, condition, warranty details, and your budget.

Negotiator AI will analyze the information and provide a recommendation.

## Limitations
- Does not independently verify live prices or stock.
- Cannot contact sellers or complete purchases.
- Recommendations depend on the information provided by the user.
- Supports laptops and smartphones.
